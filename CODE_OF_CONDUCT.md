# Code of conduct

Be kind and be useful. This is a small project and we want it to stay pleasant to contribute to.

- Treat everyone with respect, whatever their background or experience.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person. Assume good intent.
- No harassment, insults, or discriminatory language or jokes.
- Don't publish other people's private information.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits and issues that break these rules, and may block repeat offenders. Report a problem by emailing the maintainer at gkuhl@aheliotech.com. Reports are kept confidential.
